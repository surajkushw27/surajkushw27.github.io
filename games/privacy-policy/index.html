Privacy Policy

MythSphere Games · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile games published by MythSphere ("we", "us", "our") on Google Play (the "Games"). It explains what information is processed when you play our Games, who processes it, and the choices you have.

By installing or playing a Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Games.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not run our own servers for the Games and we do not store your personal data.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • Our Games show ads through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners collect some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Some Games may offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.
  • Our Games are intended for players aged 13 and older.

2. Information we collect ourselves

None. We do not collect, receive or store personal information on our own systems. Game data such as your level, coins, unlocked items and settings is stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. How the information is used

  • To show ads, which keep our Games free to play.
  • To show personalised ads where you have allowed it, or non-personalised ads where you have not.
  • To give you the rewards you earn by choosing to watch a rewarded ad.
  • To detect fraud and keep the ad service working.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose other than those listed above.

5. Your choices

  • Consent screen: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings screen ("Privacy choices").
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Privacy › Ads (the exact path differs between phones). Deleting it stops ad personalisation based on that ID.
  • Google ad settings: you can manage ad personalisation for your Google account at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Stop all processing: uninstall the Game. This removes the locally stored game data.

6. Children

Our Games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through one of our Games, please contact us and we will help you with the request.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not store personal data, we have nothing to delete on our side. Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Data held by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies, and you can ask Google to access or delete it through your Google account.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. Since the data described above is held by Google and its partners, the fastest way to use these rights is through the choices in section 5 and through your Google account. You can also email us and we will do what we can to help.

9. Security

The Games do not send personal data to us. Data sent to the third-party services above is transmitted by their software using encrypted connections.

10. International transfers

Google and its partners may process data on servers outside your country. They are responsible for the safeguards that apply to those transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date of the last update at the top. If you keep playing after a change, you accept the updated policy.
